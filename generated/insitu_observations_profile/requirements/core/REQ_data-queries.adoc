[[req_insitu_observations_profile_data_queries]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/insitu_observations_profile/data-queries
statement:: The insitu-observations collection SHALL support locations, area, and radius data queries.
part:: The collection SHALL provide a /collections/insitu-observations/locations endpoint.
part:: The collection SHALL provide a /collections/insitu-observations/area endpoint.
part:: The collection SHALL provide a /collections/insitu-observations/radius endpoint.
part:: The radius query variables SHALL include within_units containing at least m.
====
