[[req_nws_connect_space_weather_collection]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/nws_connect/space-weather-collection
statement:: The space_weather collection SHALL provide GeoJSON features for NOAA SWPC space weather alerts.
part:: The service SHALL provide a /collections/space_weather endpoint.
part:: The service SHALL provide a /collections/space_weather/items endpoint.
part:: The response Content-Type SHALL be application/geo+json.
part:: Each feature SHALL include message_type, noaa_scale, scale_category, and scale_level properties.
====
