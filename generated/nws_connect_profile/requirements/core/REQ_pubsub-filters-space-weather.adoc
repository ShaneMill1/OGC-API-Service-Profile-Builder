[[req_nws_connect_pubsub_filters_space_weather]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/nws_connect/pubsub-filters-space-weather
statement:: The space_weather collection pub/sub SHALL support filtering by message type and NOAA scale.
part:: Subscriptions SHALL accept message_type filter (Alert, Warning, Watch, Summary).
part:: Subscriptions SHALL accept scale_category filter (G, S, R).
part:: Subscriptions SHALL accept scale_level filter (minimum NOAA scale level 1-5).
part:: Subscriptions SHALL accept email filter for notification delivery.
====
