[[req_nws_connect_water_gauge_collection]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/nws_connect/water-gauge-collection
statement:: The water_gauge collection SHALL provide GeoJSON features with flood stage information.
part:: The service SHALL provide a /collections/water_gauge endpoint.
part:: The service SHALL provide a /collections/water_gauge/items endpoint.
part:: The response Content-Type SHALL be application/geo+json.
part:: Each feature SHALL include flood_stage, feet_above_stage, and value properties.
part:: Each feature geometry SHALL be a GeoJSON Point.
====
