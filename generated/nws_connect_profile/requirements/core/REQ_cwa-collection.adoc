[[req_nws_connect_cwa_collection]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/nws_connect/cwa-collection
statement:: The cwa collection SHALL provide GeoJSON features for NWS County Warning Areas.
part:: The service SHALL provide a /collections/cwa endpoint.
part:: The service SHALL provide a /collections/cwa/items endpoint.
part:: The response Content-Type SHALL be application/geo+json.
part:: Each feature SHALL include a CWA identifier property.
part:: Each feature geometry SHALL be a GeoJSON Polygon or MultiPolygon.
====
