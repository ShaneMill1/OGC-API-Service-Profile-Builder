[[req_nws_connect_pubsub_amqp]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/nws_connect/pubsub-amqp
statement:: The service SHALL support AMQP 1.0 and MQTT pub/sub for real-time notifications on water_gauge, wwa, and space_weather collections.
part:: The service SHALL provide an AMQP 1.0 broker (edr-broker) on port 5672 (RabbitMQ 4.x native AMQP 1.0).
part:: The service SHALL provide an MQTT broker (edr-broker) on port 1883 over the same amq.topic exchange.
part:: Clients SHALL be able to consume from a queue bound to amq.topic with binding key collections.water_gauge.items.
part:: Clients SHALL be able to consume from a queue bound to amq.topic with binding key collections.wwa.items.
part:: Clients SHALL be able to consume from a queue bound to amq.topic with binding key collections.space_weather.items.
part:: Clients SHALL authenticate with SASL PLAIN using their account email as the username and a named WebSocket key (wsk_...) as the password.
part:: Notifications SHALL be published as GeoJSON features.
====
