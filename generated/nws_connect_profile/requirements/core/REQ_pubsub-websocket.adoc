[[req_nws_connect_pubsub_websocket]]
[requirement]
====
[%metadata]
identifier:: /req/nws_connect/pubsub-websocket
statement:: The service SHALL support WebSocket pub/sub endpoints for browser clients.
part:: The service SHALL provide a WebSocket endpoint at ws://205.156.8.80:15670 for local development.
part:: The service SHALL provide a secure WebSocket endpoint at wss://edr-api-c.mdl.nws.noaa.gov/wifs_wss/ for production.
part:: WebSocket connections SHALL support AMQP protocol over WebSocket.
====
